# The Carousel

The 7 slides from the IG and LinkedIn carousel that introduced this ritual, plus the caption that ran with it. Just the text. So you can read it without scrolling through the post, or copy lines into your own version.

## What's in this folder

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [`slides.md`](slides.md) | The 7 slides as text. Cover, 5 action slides, CTA. |
| [`caption.md`](caption.md) | The IG and LinkedIn caption that ran with the carousel. |

## Why this exists

The carousel teaches the ritual visually. This folder lets you read the same content as plain text, which is faster to scan, easier to copy, and survives the post (IG posts get archived, repos don't).

The deeper version of the ritual lives in [`../CHECKLIST.md`](../CHECKLIST.md) at the repo root. The slides are the lean version, the checklist is the depth.
