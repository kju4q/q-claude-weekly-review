## How this caption is structured

- **Hook line** (~95 chars, visible in feed before "...more"): "i used to start every Monday from a blank slate, re-explaining myself to Claude like we hadn't talked in years"
- **Personal pain point**: makes the caption a story instead of a duplicate of the slides
- **Thesis**: "your AI doesn't compound automatically, it compounds because you maintain the files it reads"
- **Punch line**: "15 minutes every Sunday, that's the whole job"
- **CTA**: lowercase, "comment WEEK and i'll send you..."
- **Hashtags**: lowercase, attached to the CTA line with no blank line separator

## Caption rules applied

- Lowercase body for the IG/LinkedIn caption style (matches the carousel slide style and Qendresa's broader voice this month)
- Prose flow with commas, not staccato periods
- Slides teach the 5 actions; caption tells the story
- Caption never repeats the slide content verbatim
